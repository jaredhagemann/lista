## Verdict

Substantial coverage, but not ready to call fully fixed. Three gaps remain in the accepted contract: billing after trial closure, bypassing recipient acceptance through existing RLS permissions, and access revocation after closure.

The implementation includes the missing director routes and onboarding, transactional transfer with expiry/cancellation/acceptance, owner deletion protection scoped to clubs with portal access, support recovery, archival closure, domain release, notification emails and web controls. The sole-guardian deletion invariant is supplied by the earlier BUG-002 work. The normal ownership-acceptance path also passed a fresh local database control probe.

## Findings

### 1. [P1] Closing an application-managed trial does not stop conversion to paid billing

Location: `apps/web/src/app/api/club/close/route.ts:63` and `supabase/migrations/20260924000001_club_ownership_and_closure.sql:479`.

The close route cancels only an existing Stripe subscription. Lista also has application-managed trials with `subscription_status = 'trialing'`, a trial expiry, and no `stripe_subscription_id`. `close_club` leaves those billing fields unchanged. The expiration job (`apps/web/src/app/api/cron/trial-expiration/route.ts`, `runExpirations`) selects exactly that state without excluding `closed_at`, then creates a paid Stripe subscription when the customer has a default payment method.

Reproduced the database portion: after closing a trial club, the exact expiration-job predicate still selected the closed organization, with its Stripe customer and `trialing` status intact. No Stripe calls or charges were made. The subsequent subscription creation is established by the existing job implementation.

Closing must end eligibility for trial conversion, and the billing jobs must exclude closed clubs. Include a closed trial with a saved payment method in regression tests; consider an expiration job already in progress when closure starts. Existing billing checkout routes also need a closed-state guard so a closed club cannot resume billing while remaining unusable.

### 2. [P1] Direct organization-membership writes bypass ownership acceptance and director cleanup

Location: `supabase/migrations/20260924000001_club_ownership_and_closure.sql:427` (`enforce_club_owner`); retained policy in `supabase/migrations/20260416000001_organization_members.sql:87`.

The service-only transfer functions enforce recipient acceptance, but the existing `org owners can manage org_members` policy still permits authenticated owners to update any column of their owner row. The new constraint checks only that an owner remains.

Reproduced as the authenticated owner: update the owner row's `profile_id` to a different login that is not an organization director. The update succeeds, deferred constraints pass, the new login becomes owner, and there are zero transfer records. No acceptance, expiry, previous-owner demotion, Stripe email update or notice occurs. The recipient in the probe had only an ordinary player roster membership.

The same policy also permits deleting a director's organization membership without invoking `remove_org_director`. A second probe confirmed that the `director` roster row remains after that deletion, preserving team-level privileges. That bypass also skips the intended team-ownership reassignment.

Restrict client mutation of organization membership so these changes go through the authorized functions, or enforce equivalent invariants for every write path. Cover direct authenticated INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE alongside RPC tests, especially owner-row identity changes and removal cleanup.

### 3. [P1] Closure freezes access grants, preventing the promised revocation behavior

Location: `supabase/migrations/20260924000001_club_ownership_and_closure.sql:153` and `:165`.

The ticket explicitly retains removal and revocation rules after closure. The blanket trigger instead rejects DELETEs on `team_members` and `channel_members`, including service-role cleanup from the existing removal action. `remove_org_director` separately refuses closed clubs too. Consequently, authorized admins cannot revoke a member's access to the closed club's retained private history without support intervention.

Reproduced: after closing a club, its authenticated owner attempted to remove an ordinary player from the roster and received `CLUB_CLOSED`. This is distinct from intentionally blocking operational edits or new membership grants.

Provide a controlled revocation path while keeping additions and operational changes blocked. Add post-closure team/group/director removal tests, verifying loss of private-group and DM reads without erasing history or shared player identities.
